> Q2.11 Before training B–D, predict in at most three sentences which model will fix which of
> the failures you found in Testing the Baseline.

Model B (symmetry augmentation) will shrink both consistency gaps, C_R more than C_M because rotated digits were the least familiar appearance, but it will not help at 2× or 3×. Model C (speed augmentation) will clearly improve 2× but stay poor at 3×, because about 10 px/frame is beyond the reach of model A's architecture whatever it is trained on. Model D (larger reach, same speed augmentation) will be the only model that also fixes 3×, possibly at a small cost at 1×, while leaving the mirror and rotation gaps roughly where model A has them.
